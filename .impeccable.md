# k3fall

## Design Context

### Users
One operator (W6JY): experienced CW-heavy ham running POTA hunts and contests
from a Mac/iPad on the same LAN as the K3. Context: shack or couch, often at
night, hands on a keyboard, radio audio in headphones. Job: see the band in
high definition, click/tune the K3, key CW — without walking to the radio.

### Brand Personality
Precise, mechanical, unhurried. The feel of good test equipment: everything
labeled, nothing decorative, dense but calm. Sibling of Carrier Wave / CW
Sweep (see carrier_wave/docs/design-language.md).

### Aesthetic Direction
Four exploratory mockups, each a committed direction:
1. `panel` — Elecraft K3 front-panel skeuomorph (dark, amber LCD, P3 waterfall)
2. `console` — fully digital, keyboard-first instrument console (dark, one accent)
3. `sweep` — Carrier Wave native (light, Apple semantic colors, capsule badges)
4. `instrument` — Rams-era lab instrument (paper-white, ink waterfall, Braun orange)

### Design Principles
- The waterfall is the hero; controls never crowd it.
- Callsigns and frequencies are always monospaced.
- Enrichment everywhere: spots on the waterfall, worked-before/NEW badges,
  source colors matching CW Sweep (RBN blue, POTA green, cluster yellow).
- Click-to-tune is the primary interaction; keyboard is the power path.
- No AI-slop tells: no neon-cyan glow, no gradient text, no accent side-stripes.
